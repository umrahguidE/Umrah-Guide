Privacy Policy

Guided Umrah · by Mhd Wasim · Last updated 5 October 2026

In short

  • No accounts, no sign-in, no ads and no analytics SDK in the app.
  • Your progress, notes and personal information stay on your own device. The developer never receives them.
  • Location and motion sensors are optional. Ritual tracking is processed on your device; the optional online map sends location-related tile requests to OpenStreetMap.
  • One exception, explained below: the optional real online map, which contacts OpenStreetMap and jsDelivr.

What is stored on your device

Guided Umrah saves the following in your device's local storage so the app works offline and remembers where you are:

  • Your Umrah progress (current step, completed Tawaf rounds and Sa'i laps, and a short undo history).
  • Your settings: language, voice guide, text size, chosen Qur'an reciter and Miqat route.
  • Anything you type into the app yourself: checklist ticks, personal duas, and the "My info & emergency" details (for example your name, hotel, emergency contacts and any medical notes).

This data is never uploaded to the developer. Ending your session resets your Umrah progress. Clear the app's data or storage, or uninstall it, to remove the other locally stored information.

Location and motion sensors

If you allow it, the app uses your phone's location (GPS), compass and step sensors to warn you when you approach your Miqat, to suggest when a Tawaf round or Sa'i lap may be finished, and to show your position on the map. Ritual tracking is processed on your device. The optional online map makes the requests described below; the developer does not receive your location. You can turn tracking off in the app, or withdraw the permission in your phone's settings, at any time. The app never marks a round or lap complete by itself: you always confirm it.

The one exception: the real online map

The Haram diagram in the app works fully offline. But if you turn on location while you are far from Masjid al-Haram (more than about 500 m), for example while trying the app at home, the app shows a real online map instead. To draw it, your device downloads:

  • in the web version only, a map library from cdn.jsdelivr.net (the Android and iOS apps have it built in), and
  • map images (tiles) from the OpenStreetMap tile servers.

Requests use HTTPS. OpenStreetMap can see your device's IP address, request times and the requested map tiles. Tiles centered on your position reveal the viewed location, including precise location at close zoom levels. OpenStreetMap may retain service logs, so this processing is not limited to temporary memory. The developer does not receive these logs. The providers handle requests under their own policies: OpenStreetMap Foundation privacy policy and, for the web map library, jsDelivr privacy policy. The offline Haram diagram does not request these map tiles.

Voice guide

The spoken guidance uses your device's built-in text-to-speech. Depending on your phone, its speech service may process the spoken sentences under its own privacy policy. The sentences are generic step instructions, not your personal information. Arabic duas and Qur'an are never read by a synthetic voice: they are played from recorded recitations bundled with the app.

Web version

If you use the app in a web browser, the pages are served by GitHub Pages, which may log visitors' IP addresses under GitHub's own privacy statement. The Android and iOS apps contain their files on the device.

Children

The developer does not receive children's progress or personal notes. Optional online map requests are handled by the providers described above.

Your control

Use "End current Umrah session" under More to reset your progress. Clear the app's storage in your phone settings or uninstall it to remove locally stored information. These actions do not delete logs retained by external map providers. For questions about their logs or privacy rights, consult their policies and contact details linked above.

Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change, this page will be updated and the date above will change.

Contact

Questions or concerns: email umrahguideapp@gmail.com. You can also report app issues at github.com/umrahguidE/Umrah-Guide/issues; do not post private information in public issues.
