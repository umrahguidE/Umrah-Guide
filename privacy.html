Privacy Policy

Guided Umrah · by Mhd Wasim · Last updated 24 September 2026

In short

  • No accounts, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics, no tracking.
  • Your progress, notes and personal information stay on your own device. The developer never receives them.
  • Location and motion sensors are used only on your device, and only when you turn them on.
  • One exception, explained below: the optional real online map, which contacts OpenStreetMap and jsDelivr.

What is stored on your device

Guided Umrah saves the following in your device's local storage so the app works offline and remembers where you are:

  • Your Umrah progress (current step, completed Tawaf rounds and Sa'i laps, and a short undo history).
  • Your settings: language, voice guide, text size, chosen Qur'an reciter and Miqat route.
  • Anything you type into the app yourself: checklist ticks, personal duas, and the "My info & emergency" details (for example your name, hotel, emergency contacts and any medical notes).

This data is never uploaded to a server. It is removed when you end your session, clear the app's data or storage, or uninstall the app.

Location and motion sensors

If you allow it, the app uses your phone's location (GPS), compass and step sensors to warn you when you approach your Miqat, to suggest when a Tawaf round or Sa'i lap may be finished, and to show your position on the map. This processing happens on your device. Your location is not sent to the developer or stored on any server. You can turn tracking off in the app, or withdraw the permission in your phone's settings, at any time. The app never marks a round or lap complete by itself: you always confirm it.

The one exception: the real online map

The Haram diagram in the app works fully offline. But if you turn on location while you are far from Masjid al-Haram (more than about 500 m), for example while trying the app at home, the app shows a real online map instead. To draw it, your device downloads:

  • a map library from cdn.jsdelivr.net, and
  • map images (tiles) from the OpenStreetMap tile servers.

Those services can see your device's IP address and which map area is requested, which reveals roughly where you are. The developer does not receive this information. They handle it under their own policies: OpenStreetMap Foundation privacy policy and jsDelivr privacy policy. Near Masjid al-Haram, and everywhere else in the app, none of this happens.

Voice guide

The spoken guidance uses your device's built-in text-to-speech. Depending on your phone, its speech service may process the spoken sentences under its own privacy policy. The sentences are generic step instructions, not your personal information. Arabic duas and Qur'an are never read by a synthetic voice: they are played from recorded recitations bundled with the app.

Web version

If you use the app in a web browser, the pages are served by GitHub Pages, which may log visitors' IP addresses under GitHub's own privacy statement. The Android and iOS apps contain their files on the device.

Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Your control

Because your data lives only on your device, you control it: use "End current Umrah session" in Settings to reset your progress, clear the app's storage in your phone settings, or uninstall the app to remove everything.

Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change, this page will be updated and the date above will change.

Contact

Questions or concerns: please open an issue at github.com/wasimnawisaadi-code/umrah-app/issues.
